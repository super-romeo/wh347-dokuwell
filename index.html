How it works

On federally funded or assisted construction covered by the Davis-Bacon and Related Acts, contractors and subcontractors submit certified payroll for every week of work on the project. Form WH-347 of the U.S. Department of Labor is an optional form for these reports, and many states add their own format for state public works.

The work repeats every week: the payroll system knows hours and pay, but not the classification and the wage determination of each project. The service takes your weekly payroll export, matches every worker to a classification of the project's wage determination and fills WH-347 per project. Hours paid below the determined rate or fringe are flagged before you sign.

You review the report, sign the Statement of Compliance and submit it yourself. Reports, determinations and exports stay in an archive by project and week.

Questions

Who has to submit certified payroll?

Contractors and subcontractors on construction covered by the Davis-Bacon and Related Acts submit it weekly; WH-347 is the Department of Labor form for it. State public works may require their own format.

Do you sign or submit the report for us?

No. You review, sign the Statement of Compliance and submit the report yourself. We are not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Labor or any state agency.

What do I need to start?

The weekly payroll export (CSV or Excel), the wage determination of each project and the classifications of your workers.

Can I use it already?

Not yet. The service is in preparation, and we are checking whether contractors need it.
